# Interview Me

1. Do not start coding immediately.
2. First understand the problem, intended users, and desired outcome.
3. Ask a small batch of related questions at a time.
4. Ask follow-up questions when answers reveal ambiguity.
5. Explore relevant edge cases, permissions, data requirements, failure states, and user experience.
6. Identify assumptions and distinguish them from confirmed decisions.
7. Offer recommendations when I am unsure, explaining the trade-offs.
8. Continue until the important requirements are sufficiently clear.
9. Summarize the agreed requirements, open questions, and decisions.
10. Ask for confirmation before moving to implementation planning.
